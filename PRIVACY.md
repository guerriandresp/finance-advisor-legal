# Finance Advisor — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 13, 2026

## 1. Overview

Finance Advisor is a private, non-commercial personal finance application developed for personal use.

The application is intended to consolidate financial information from bank accounts and investment platforms into a single dashboard.

Finance Advisor is not offered as a public service and does not accept registrations from external users.

## 2. Information Accessed

With the account holder's explicit authorization, Finance Advisor may access:

- Bank account balances and currencies.
- Bank transactions, including dates, amounts and descriptions.
- Account identifiers required to associate financial records with the correct account.
- Investment holdings, valuations and transaction history, where supported.

The specific information available depends on the connected financial institution and its API.

## 3. Purpose of Data Processing

Financial information is accessed exclusively for personal financial management, including:

- Consolidating account balances and investment holdings.
- Monitoring income, expenses and transfers.
- Tracking portfolio allocation and historical performance.
- Generating personal financial reports and analyses.

Finance Advisor does not initiate payments, transfer money or execute investment transactions.

## 4. Data Access and Authorization

Banking connections may be established through authorized Open Banking providers, including Enable Banking.

Access to banking information requires the account holder's explicit consent and is limited to the permissions granted by the financial institution.

The account holder can revoke banking access through the relevant financial institution or connection provider.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Finance Advisor is designed to operate as a private application.

The intended security practices include:

- Keeping private cryptographic keys and authentication credentials outside public repositories.
- Restricting access to financial information to the application owner.
- Using read-only integrations wherever supported.
- Avoiding the public disclosure of financial records and account credentials.

Financial data may be processed and stored locally to support the application's functionality. Storage, retention and deletion arrangements will depend on the implemented features.

## 6. Data Sharing

Finance Advisor does not sell financial information or use it for advertising.

Financial institutions and authorized connection providers may process information as necessary to establish and maintain authorized banking connections, subject to their respective privacy policies.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

The application owner controls the local financial data maintained by Finance Advisor.

Stored information may be deleted when it is no longer required. Revoking a banking connection prevents further authorized access but does not automatically delete information already stored locally.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as Finance Advisor develops or its data processing practices change.

## 9. Contact

For questions concerning this application or its privacy practices, please contact the developer through the Finance Advisor legal documentation repository on GitHub.
