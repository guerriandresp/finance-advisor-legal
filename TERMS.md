# Finance Advisor — Terms of Use

**Last updated:** October 13, 2026

## 1. About Finance Advisor

Finance Advisor is a private, non-commercial personal financial management application.

Its purpose is to consolidate and analyze financial information from accounts owned or controlled by the application developer.

Finance Advisor is not publicly available as a commercial service.

## 2. Authorized Use

Finance Advisor is intended exclusively for personal use.

The application may connect to supported financial institutions and authorized data aggregation providers, subject to their applicable terms and authorization requirements.

Only accounts for which the application owner has the necessary authorization may be connected.

## 3. Banking and Investment Integrations

Finance Advisor may retrieve financial information through supported APIs, including Open Banking services provided by Enable Banking.

Access to financial information requires appropriate authorization and may be subject to additional conditions imposed by financial institutions or API providers.

Finance Advisor is designed for read-only access and does not initiate bank payments, transfer funds or execute investment transactions.

## 4. Financial Information

The application may display balances, transactions, investment holdings, historical performance and other financial information.

Information may be delayed, incomplete or subject to inaccuracies arising from external data sources.

Finance Advisor does not guarantee the accuracy, completeness or real-time availability of financial information.

## 5. No Financial Advice

Finance Advisor is a personal financial analysis tool.

Any calculations, projections or recommendations generated by the application are intended for personal informational purposes only.

The application does not provide regulated financial, investment, legal or tax advisory services.

## 6. Security and Responsibility

The application owner is responsible for protecting authentication credentials, private keys and access to the application.

Financial connections should use secure and authorized authentication mechanisms.

Access to connected accounts can be revoked through the relevant financial institution or service provider.

## 7. Third-Party Services

Finance Advisor may rely on external financial institutions, API providers and Open Banking intermediaries.

These services operate under their own terms, privacy policies and technical limitations.

Finance Advisor is not affiliated with or endorsed by these institutions unless explicitly stated.

## 8. Availability and Changes

Finance Advisor is an independently developed personal project.

Its functionality, integrations and availability may change without notice.
